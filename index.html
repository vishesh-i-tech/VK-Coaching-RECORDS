What happens when you rely on notebooks:

  • Slow record searches when parents visit

    When a parent arrives at your desk asking about their child's attendance and marks, you spend five awkward minutes flipping through multiple registers.

  • Fee dues slip through the cracks

    When fees are scribbled in margins, missed months go unnoticed. By the time you realize a student has not paid, two months have already passed.

  • Attendance steals 15 minutes of class time

    Calling out thirty-five names every single day wastes valuable minutes that you could have spent teaching a difficult concept or solving questions.

  • Paper tears, gets lost, and cannot be backed up

    A single spilled cup of tea or a misplaced register can wipe out an entire year of attendance, fee logs, and student performance records.
